Privacy / 隱私權

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Game data

Opposite Cats stores puzzle progress and settings on your device. It has no account system, custom server, or custom analytics service. Gameplay works offline.

Advertising

Google AdMob supplies ads. Its SDK may process an IP address to estimate general location, crash and performance information, device identifiers, advertising data, and interactions with ads and the app. Google uses these for advertising, measurement, analytics, and service improvement. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's SDK data disclosure.

Where required, a consent form offers privacy choices before ads can be requested. If Google requires ongoing privacy controls, an ad privacy option appears in Settings. Available ads depend on consent, region, connectivity, and supply.

Purchases and sharing

Apple processes in-app purchases and provides verified purchase entitlements so the game can unlock permanent products. We do not receive your full payment details. Sharing a puzzle card opens your device's share sheet; you choose the recipient or app.

Your choices

Rewarded ads are optional. Comfort removes all ads. You can change available ad privacy choices in the game's Settings and tracking permissions in iOS Settings. Removing the app deletes its local game data; this does not cancel permanent purchases, which can be restored through Apple. Third-party data retention is governed by the provider's policy.

繁體中文

關卡進度和設定儲存在你的裝置，遊戲沒有帳號、自建伺服器或自訂分析服務，可離線遊玩。

廣告由 Google AdMob 提供。其 SDK 可能處理 IP 推算的大概位置、當機及效能資料、裝置識別碼、廣告資料，以及廣告和應用程式互動，用於廣告、成效分析及服務改善。適用地區會先提供同意選項；如需持續提供隱私控制，遊戲設定會顯示廣告隱私選項。詳情請參閱上方 Google 隱私政策。

獎勵廣告可自由選擇，舒玩版可永久移除廣告。Apple 處理付款；遊戲只使用已驗證的購買權益解鎖商品，不會收到完整付款資料。分享卡由你在系統分享選單選擇目的地。刪除應用程式會移除本機遊戲資料，但不會取消永久購買；你可透過 Apple 恢復購買。
