Privacy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Game data

Opposite Cats stores puzzle progress and settings on your device. The game has no account system and no custom server.

Advertising and purchases

If advertising is enabled for a release, the advertising provider may process device and ad interaction data under its own privacy terms. Apple processes in-app purchases. Opposite Cats does not receive your full payment details.

Your choices

You can limit ad tracking in iOS settings, decline optional rewarded ads, and delete local game data by removing the app. Ads never block offline play.
